Recorded VAT position

Only recorded VAT invoices and eligible purchase VAT below are included. Other earnings, receipt scans, regular costs and catch-up totals are not automatically classified for VAT. Review missing records. This is not a VAT return or HMRC submission; income tax remains a separate estimate.

Record eligible purchase VAT

Your recorded expense must already exclude private spending. Enter only VAT attributable to that recorded business expense.

Do not automatically reclaim vehicle, fuel or private-use VAT. This form supports ordinary UK purchases at rates up to 20%; special rules, imports and reverse charges are not supported.
